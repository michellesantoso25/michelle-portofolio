BACKGROUND

My journey so far

I'm a Computer Science student specializing in Software Engineering at BINUS University with a focus on full-stack web and mobile application development. Truth be told, I entered university genuinely clueless about what I wanted to study. At first, I considered pharmacy, medicine, and several other paths before landing on Computer Science. I had zero programming background, making the decision equal parts exciting and intimidating.

The first few months were a tough survival process where I felt constantly behind and wondered if I had made the wrong call. However, as I persisted, that hesitation turned into real passion. I began enjoying the challenge of building applications, thinking through complex logic, and catching edge cases early to deliver smooth user experiences.

As an extrovert, I thrive in collaborative team environments, enjoying the process of discussing ideas and tackling problems from different angles. Across my projects, I gravitate most toward UI/UX design and translating those visuals into reliable, fully functioning backend and frontend systems.
